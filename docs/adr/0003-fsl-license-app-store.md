# Source-available under FSL-1.1-MIT, sold on the App Store

The repo is public, but ShowRecorder is meant to be a paid App Store app (one Universal Purchase listing for iPhone, iPad and Mac). Under MIT, anyone could ship a copy. Under GPL, sole-owner relicensing would be needed for the App Store. PolyForm Noncommercial never becomes open source. We chose the Functional Source License (FSL-1.1-MIT): anyone may read, change and use the code for anything except building a competing product, and each release becomes MIT two years after it is published.

## Consequences

- Outside contributions need a contributor agreement (or at least an inbound licensing note) so that we stay able to ship on the App Store.
