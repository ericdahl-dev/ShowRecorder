# One-time Pro unlock, a channel-limited Free tier, and the Show-Safe Promise

ShowRecorder is free to download with a 14-day full Trial (a $0 non-consumable in-app purchase, App Review Guideline 3.1.1). After the Trial, Free records 2 USB Channels of the operator's choice, with Source names and colors; a one-time Pro unlock ($29.99, Universal Purchase, Family Sharing on) records every USB Channel and adds the Show report, Reaper export, Templates and Mixer Triggers. We chose a channel gate over the obvious 30-minute recording cap because a cap that stops a live set loses audio that can't be recorded again, and locking files after a limit holds the operator's show hostage. We chose a one-time unlock over a subscription because X-Air users already pay that way (Mixing Station) and the app costs almost nothing per user to run.

The **Show-Safe Promise** binds every limit: a Take never stops, and audio is never withheld, because of a Trial, limit or license. Limits are checked only when record is pressed; a Trial that expires during a Take records to the end.

## Consequences

- Family Sharing can't be turned off for an in-app purchase once enabled; a church team on one family plan shares one unlock.
- Apple's volume purchasing doesn't cover in-app purchases. If organizations need purchase orders, that needs a paid-upfront option later (a separate listing risks Guideline 4.3).
- Future paid features (X32/M32 support, virtual soundcheck, AAF/Logic export) are separate add-ons or an optional subscription, never taken away from Pro.
