New in this build: fixes to how a Take starts and stops around an interruption, and safer Drive Copies. Free records every USB Channel; Pro adds names and colors from the mixer, the second Copy on a Drive, unlimited Markers, auto-gain and clip protection. The 14-day Trial includes everything.

New since 1.1.13:
- A Take pressed while input is stopped (a call, Siri) starts its silence at the press, and input comes back without leaving the app.
- Record then Stop before any audio arrives no longer adds stray audio to the next Take.
- Drive only: a slow Drive in one Take, or a short interruption, no longer makes this iPhone stand in for the next Take.
- Audio that only this iPhone holds is never removed until the Drive is checked to hold all of it.
- Repair records its result on the Drive before it removes anything from this iPhone.
- A Drive that fails while saving no longer damages the first sample of a Stem.
- A Drive pulled just as a Take starts gets readable files when it rejoins, and Repair carries on with the other channels.

What to try, the riskiest first:
1. While Armed, start Siri or take a call so input stops, press Record, wait about 30 seconds, then end it. Does the Take start with about 30 seconds of silence marked as a Dropout, and do the meters come back on their own?
2. Right after plugging in the mixer, press Record and then Stop before the meters move. Then record a normal Take and place a Marker. Is the Marker where you put it, and is the Take the right length?
3. Drive only, healthy Drive: start Siri for a second mid-Take. Does this iPhone stay on standby, not standing in?
4. Drive only: record to a slow USB stick until this iPhone stands in, Stop, then record another Take to a fast Drive. Does this iPhone stay on standby the whole Take?
5. Record to Both and pull the Drive mid-Take, then plug it back in. Does the Take end complete on the Drive, with no Gaps?
