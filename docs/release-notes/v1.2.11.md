New in this build: Mixer Link for the Behringer WING (WING, WING Rack, WING Compact). It is here so a tester with a real WING can try it; we have never connected to a real WING. Everything in 1.2.10 is here too.

New since 1.2.10:
- Mixer Link finds a WING on the network and names each of its 48 USB Channels after the Source patched to it, with that Source's color.
- A rename or re-patch on the WING shows up within a few seconds: ShowRecorder reads the WING's names again every 5 seconds. A rename during a Take goes into the Take's record and the Show report, and a re-patch adds a marker in the Reaper project.
- If the WING doesn't answer Mixer Link, the USB Channels keep plain numbered names and recording works as usual.

What to try, the riskiest first (checked only by tests, CI builds and a WING emulator that answers only discovery, never with a real WING, on a device or in the Simulator):
1. With the XR18, record a short Take as usual. Does Mixer Link still find the XR18, and do names and the Take behave as in 1.2.10? The new WING search must not get in the way.
2. With the 18- and 32-channel Demo signal, nothing about the WING should show anywhere.
3. With a WING (for the WING tester): do all 48 USB Channels show on the iPhone or iPad? Does Mixer Link find the WING and name and color each channel after its Source? Rename a channel on the WING: does the new name show within a few seconds?
4. The 1.2.10 list still stands: 32 meters line up, 32-channel Demo signal, slow Drive Stop, pulling the stick while it saves, clip protection and the no-signal warning, iPad hold to stop, Settings > Help, and sharing off sends nothing.
