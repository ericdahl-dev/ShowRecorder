New since 0.1.15:

- Mixer renames show up live. While Armed, renaming a channel on the mixer updates the meters within a few seconds. During a Take the files keep their names, and each rename is listed in the Show report with when it happened.
- Appearance is a dropdown again (Settings > Recording > Appearance).
- Stop and Marker are further apart, so a Marker tap can't land on Stop. The Marker button is easier to see while Armed.
- Every meter is the same height when a channel has a name (a typed name used to shorten its own meter).
- The Warm heat chip is just its thermometer, so the Device, Drive and Mixer chips keep their words.
- Show list: a separate message if the Shows folder can't be read, and "No Takes yet" in an empty Show.

What to try:

1. Settings > Recording > Appearance: pick Sunlight, then Dark, from the dropdown. Does it switch each time?
2. With your mixer linked over Wi-Fi and the app Armed, rename a channel on the mixer. Does the meter label change within a few seconds? Rename one during a Take, stop, and open the Show report.
3. Name channel 1 (tap its number), and check all 18 meters are the same height.
4. Tap Marker during a Take: is it easy to hit without coming near Stop?
