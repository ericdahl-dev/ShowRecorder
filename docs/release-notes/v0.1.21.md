New since 0.1.20:

- Settings opens as a full sheet again on iPhone. Markers and Channel names stay as panels, so Stop and Marker stay in reach during a Take.
- Templates: a star on each row makes it the default; no more swiping to find it.
- The record screen shows the Show's total time next to the Take's, and the Mixer chip names your mixer ("Skeyelab XR18").
- With nothing armed, the meter area says why and offers the one button that fixes it.
- If a call, Siri or another app interrupts a Take, the time it missed is kept as silence with a Dropout marker, so your files stay in sync with video.
- A slow Drive no longer loses audio the phone has: the gap is filled in after the Take. A Drive that hangs no longer holds up Stop.
- The "Stop recording?" question closes by itself if the Take has already ended.
- If the mixer restarts or the Wi-Fi drops, the Mixer Link comes back on its own, without Retry.
- VoiceOver reads meters in plain levels, says the channel names, and announces new alerts, clips and Dropouts. Very large text sizes are capped on the record screen so the meters keep their height.

What to try:

1. Open Settings during a Take: is it a full sheet, and can you get back to Stop easily? Open Markers: is Stop still in reach?
2. Tap the star on a Template: does it become the default (★)?
3. With the XR18 linked, does the Mixer chip say "Skeyelab XR18"? Is the Show's total time shown?
4. Start a Take, take or make a short phone call, and stop. Does the Take's length match the time on the clock, with a Dropout where the call was?
5. With the Link up, power-cycle the XR18: does the Link come back by itself?
6. If you use VoiceOver: do the meters and alerts read sensibly?
