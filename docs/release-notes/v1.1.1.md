New: Behringer X32 and Midas M32 over Mixer Link. ShowRecorder reads the desk's channel names and colors for all 32 USB Channels, and names each Stem after the channel that is actually patched to that USB channel, not just USB 1 = channel 1. The XR18 and MR18 now do the same: each USB Channel is named after what the mixer really routes to it.

This is the first build that talks to a real X32 or M32. If you have one, please try it and tell us what you see, good or bad.

What to try on an X32 or M32:

1. Connect the desk's USB card to your iPhone, iPad or Mac, and join the desk's network. Does the app record from it, and does Settings show 32 USB Channels?
2. Open Settings, Mixer Link, and Find Mixers. Does it find your desk? Once linked, do the meters show the desk's channel names and colors?
3. Rename a channel or change its color on the desk. Does the app follow within a second or two?
4. If you've re-patched the USB card's outputs (for example, a bus or a different channel on USB 1), record a short Take. Are the files named after what's really on each USB channel?
5. Record a Take and check the files in the Files app or on the drive: one WAV per USB Channel, named as on the desk.

On an XR18 or MR18: if you route something other than the default to a USB channel, record a short Take and check the file is named after what's routed there.

Also new: on Free, the Channel names page says that files use the Mixer's names. Pro puts your typed names on the files.
