New since 0.1.9:

- Name channels by hand. Tap a channel's label on the record screen (while Armed or during a Take), type a name and Save. The files take the name when the Take ends. Names stay for the rest of the Show.
- Channel names page. Under the Show name, tap "Channel names" (or Names in landscape; also in Settings). One row per channel, with chips for common names (Kick, Snare, Vox 1, Vox 2...), names you typed before, and "Copy from last Show".
- Share a Show. On the Show list, swipe a Show right (or long-press it) and tap Share to AirDrop it or save it to Files. The Show detail has a Share button too.
- Delete. On the Show list, swipe a Show left (or long-press) for Delete..., and on a Show's detail delete one Take or one channel's file. You choose the Device copy, the Drive copy or both, and type the Show's name to confirm. Deleting is permanent.
- Copy status line now says "(no Drive)" when there is no Drive copy, and counts Dropouts.

What to try:

1. Record a short Take, tap a channel label during it, name it, stop. Check the file is named after the channel.
2. Open Channel names, tap chips for a few rows, then start a new Show and use "Copy from last Show".
3. Share a Show to your Mac with AirDrop, and check the Stems, Report.html and the Reaper project arrive.
4. Delete a throwaway Show from the Show list. Do not delete a real recording to test this.
5. Confirm the Demo signal still shows as an input.
