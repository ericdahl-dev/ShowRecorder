New in this build: 32 channels fit on the record screen. Everything in 1.2.8 is here too.

New since 1.2.8:
- With 32 USB Channels (an X32, or the 32-channel Demo signal), every meter now fits across the iPhone screen instead of pushing the record screen off both sides. Meters stay full height in one row; with many channels they get narrower and the gaps shrink.
- With 18 channels the meters look as before. With one or two channels a meter no longer stretches across the whole screen.

What to try, the riskiest first (the screen fix was checked only by tests and CI builds, never on a device or in the Simulator):
1. Pick "Demo signal (32 channels)" on the iPhone, in portrait. Do all 32 meters fit, with the header, the status chips and the Take line on screen? Can you read the channel numbers? Turn the phone to landscape and check again.
2. Record a short Take with the 32-channel Demo signal and make one channel clip, or leave one out by long-pressing its meter. Do the clip and not-ready marks show in the narrow lanes?
3. With the XR18 (18 channels), does the record screen look the same as in 1.2.8, in portrait and landscape?
4. Record with the built-in microphone (one or two channels). Are the meters a sensible width, centered?
5. Same checks on the iPad and the Mac, if you can.
6. The 1.2.8 list still stands: slow Drive Stop, pulling the stick while it saves, the X32 pack offered first with the 32-channel Demo signal, clip protection and the no-signal warning, iPad hold to stop, both Demo signals in Airplane Mode, Settings > Help, and sharing off sends nothing.
