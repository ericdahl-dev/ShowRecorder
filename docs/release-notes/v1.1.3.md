New: an Apple Watch app. With ShowRecorder recording on your iPhone, the watch shows Armed or Recording with the time so far, and lets you drop Markers, start and stop from your wrist. It's free, and it's in this build for the first time, so please try it if you have a watch.

Also in this build:
- If the mixer's USB routing changes during a Take (a USB Channel now carries another channel), the record screen warns you and a Marker marks the moment. The Take carries on and its Stems keep their names.
- The Marker list shows gain and routing Markers as themselves, not as "Dropout".
- The Drive shows by its name, not a long code.
- Mixer Link problems are in plain words, not "NWError error 22".
- On the X32 and M32, an X-LIVE card is recognized as well as the X-USB.
- Arming again after a call, a change of audio interface or route, or a settings change is more reliable.

What to try, the riskiest first:

The Apple Watch app (new in this build):
1. Install ShowRecorder on your watch from the Watch app on your iPhone, if it isn't there already.
2. Arm on the iPhone, then open ShowRecorder on the watch. Does it say Armed? Tap Record. Does a Take start on the iPhone?
3. While recording, tap Marker on the watch (or double tap, on a watch that has it). Does the watch tap your wrist, and does the Marker appear on the iPhone, once per tap?
4. Stop: press and hold the stop button for a second, until the ring fills. Letting go early should do nothing.
5. Lock the iPhone during a Take and drop a Marker from the watch. Does it still land?
6. Walk away from the iPhone until the watch says "No link to iPhone", then come back. The Take on the iPhone should carry on throughout, and nothing you tapped while out of reach should happen later.
7. With the iPhone not Armed, the watch should say to open ShowRecorder on the iPhone.
8. During a Take, the Recording status should also show in the watch's Smart Stack.

Recording itself (Arm, record and stop were rebuilt in this build):
9. Arm, record a short Take, stop. Are the files all there?
10. During a Take, unplug the mixer's USB cable, then plug it back in. The Take should end with a message saying why, and the app should Arm again on its own once the cable is back.
11. Take a phone call (or have someone call you) during a Take. When the call ends, does recording carry on, and does the record screen say what happened?
12. Change the Pre-roll or sample rate in Settings between Takes. Does the next Take use it?

Also: if you re-patch a channel on the mixer during a Take, check for the warning and the Marker.
