New in this build: a Take's WAV time stamps match the moment you pressed Record, even when the Take starts late, and a Drive that fails as it joins no longer reports dropped frames that never happened. Free records every USB Channel; Pro adds names and colors from the mixer, the second Copy on a Drive, unlimited Markers, auto-gain and clip protection. The 14-day Trial includes everything.

New since 1.1.15:
- Each Stem's time stamp is the moment you pressed Record, minus Pre-roll, and its audio starts there too, even when the Drive was slow to start.
- A Drive that fails as it joins a running Take gets no more audio, so the Take report no longer lists dropped frames that never happened.
- Changing Pre-roll or the sample rate while a Take is being saved no longer shows "Couldn't start".
- Changing plans while a Take is being saved Arms again once the save ends.
- Renaming a Marker or repairing a Take no longer damages a Stem that a DAW saved again.
- Repair and a stand-in move write the same Marker cue points as the recording.
- A line break in a channel or Marker name no longer breaks the Reaper project.
- Channels.csv: a name that starts with =, +, - or @ opens in a spreadsheet as text, not as a formula.
- Drive only: a Take no longer lists a Device Dropout for an interruption the Device never held.
- Setting the iPhone's date back no longer extends the Trial.
- On the Free plan, the watch no longer confirms a Marker past the limit, and says why.
- Mixer Link ignores malformed network messages instead of crashing.

What to try, the riskiest first (the first three change how a Take records; Drive only needs Pro or the Trial):
1. Slow stick, Drive only, every channel: set Settings › Storage › Record to: the Drive and press Record. Does it say "Starting…" for no more than about 1.5 seconds, with the screen staying smooth? After Stop and the move, open a Stem in a DAW that reads WAV time stamps: does it start at the time you pressed Record, minus Pre-roll, with no gap or jump at the start?
2. Start a Take recording to Both, then plug the Drive in while it records and pull it out again within a second or two. Does the Take report show no dropped frames for the Drive?
3. Stop a Take and, while it is still saving, change Pre-roll or the sample rate. Does the app Arm again with no "Couldn't start" message?
4. Rename a Marker on a Take whose Stems you opened and saved in a DAW on the Mac. Do the Stems still play in full?
5. Name a channel with a line break or starting with "=", then open the Reaper project and Channels.csv on the Mac. Do both open cleanly?
