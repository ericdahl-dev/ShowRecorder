New in this build: a 32-channel Demo signal, for trying a 32-channel Take and the X32 pack without an X32. Everything in 1.2.7 is here too.

New since 1.2.7:
- Settings > Audio interface offers "Demo signal (32 channels)" next to the 18-channel one, in TestFlight builds only. It sends moving levels on 31 channels and leaves the last one silent, like the 18-channel one.
- For the mixer packs, the 32-channel Demo signal counts as an X32, the way the 18-channel one counts as an X Air. Mixer Link stays off on both.

What to try, the riskiest first (checked only by tests and CI builds, never on a device or in the Simulator):
1. Use the slow USB stick as the Drive, 18 channels, Pro. Record a minute, then Stop. Does the screen say "Saving to the Drive… Don't unplug." and wait, and does every Stem on the stick open with its full length afterward?
2. Same setup, but pull the stick while it says "Saving to the Drive". Plug it back in. Are the Stems finished and their Gaps Repaired?
3. Pick "Demo signal (32 channels)" and record a Take of a minute or two, to the Drive if you can. Do the meters show 32 channels, and are there 32 Stems at full length, the last one silent?
4. On Free, with the 32-channel Demo signal Ready, open the upgrade offer. Is the X32 pack offered first? Then pick the 18-channel Demo signal: is the X Air pack offered first, as before?
5. With the XR18, record a Take that clips one channel, then one with a channel silent. Do clip protection and the no-signal warning still work?
6. On the iPad, hold to stop, with VoiceOver on if you can.
7. Turn on Airplane Mode, then open Settings > Support. Are both Demo signals still offered?
8. Tap Settings > Help. Do the docs open?
9. Turn "Share crash reports and usage" off, record a short Take, and check that nothing new reaches PostHog.
