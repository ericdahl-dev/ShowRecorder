New in this build: the new Free and Pro. Free now records every USB Channel. Pro adds what makes a show easier: channel names and colors from the mixer on your Stems, the second Copy on a Drive, unlimited Markers, auto-gain and clip protection. The 14-day Trial still includes everything, so you'll only see the Free limits once your Trial has ended.

Also in this build:
- Stop a Take on the iPhone or iPad by holding Stop for a second, like on the Apple Watch. A red ring fills while you hold; a tap only shows "Hold to stop". The Mac still asks before it stops.
- The Apple Watch reconnects by itself when you raise your wrist, instead of staying on "No link" until you reopen the app.
- Green banners, like the Copy summary after a Take, fade after 10 seconds. Tap × or swipe one away sooner. Warnings stay until they're fixed.
- Place a Marker from the Lock Screen or the Dynamic Island while a Take is running, without unlocking.
- The Apple Watch no longer quits when you press Record, Marker or Stop on it.
- When the iPhone is locked or ShowRecorder is in the background, the watch shows the last status it heard, dimmed with how long ago.
- The Trial chip reads "Trial · 12 days"; tap it to see what the Trial includes and what happens after.
- In portrait, the Drive and warning chips stay readable; quieter chips shrink to an icon first.
- On Free, a Drive you plug in shows "Drive · Pro" and the Take is recorded on this iPhone; you're offered Pro once, never during a Take.
- On Free, a Take keeps up to 3 Markers: placing the 3rd shows a short note under the Marker button, and a 4th press places nothing.
- After a Free Take with Mixer Link, you're offered Pro, at most once a Show, to name the Stems from the mixer.
- Settings has a Contact support row, with your app and device details filled in for you to check before sending.

What to try, the riskiest first:
1. Start a Take, let the Apple Watch go to sleep, then raise your wrist. Does it show the Take again within a second or two, without reopening the app? Try it with the iPhone locked too.
2. Tap Stop once during a Take: does it keep recording and show "Hold to stop"? Then hold Stop: does the ring fill and the Take stop?
3. After a Take, does the green Copy summary fade after about 10 seconds? Next time, swipe it away, and once tap ×.
4. Start a Take and lock the iPhone. Tap the Marker button on the Lock Screen. Does it place one Marker without unlocking? Try it from the Dynamic Island too.
5. On the Apple Watch, press Record, drop a Marker and hold Stop. Does the watch app stay open?

Free (once your 14-day Trial has ended):
6. Record a Take. Are all the USB Channels there, named USB 01, USB 02 and so on?
7. Plug in a Drive. Does the chip read "Drive · Pro", and does the Take land on this iPhone only?
8. Drop 4 Markers in one Take, on the record screen or the Lock Screen. Does the note appear at the 3rd, and does the 4th press place nothing?

Pro or the Trial:
9. Record a Take with Mixer Link on. Do the Stems have the mixer's channel names, and does the Drive get its Copy?
