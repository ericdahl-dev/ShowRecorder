New in this build: a Drive that drops out and comes back during a Take, or is plugged in just as you press Stop, is handled more safely. Free records every USB Channel; Pro adds names and colors from the mixer, the second Copy on a Drive, unlimited Markers, auto-gain and clip protection. The 14-day Trial includes everything.

New since 1.1.16:
- A Drive that fails and comes back within a split second joins the Take again, with its Stems lined up with this iPhone's.
- Pressing Stop just as a Drive joins a Take no longer keeps writing to that Drive after Stop.

What to try, the riskiest first (both change how a Take records; a Drive Copy needs Pro or the Trial):
1. Start a Take recording to Both with the Drive plugged in. Pull the Drive out and plug it straight back in, a few times. After Stop, do the Drive's Stems line up with this iPhone's in a DAW, and does the Take report show no dropped frames that didn't happen?
2. Start a Take recording to Both without the Drive, then plug it in and press Stop at the same moment. Does the Take finish saving normally, with the Drive's Stems complete?
