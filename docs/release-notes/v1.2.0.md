New in this build: the first 1.2 test build. TestFlight builds now send crash reports and usage to the developer, and you can turn that off in Settings.

New since 1.1.18:
- Crash reports and usage: when the app crashes, a report goes to the developer's own error server, and a short list of usage events goes to PostHog: the app opened, a Take started or stopped (its length, number of channels, where it recorded, the mixer family, any Dropouts), a Show created or deleted, a Drive connected or lost, Mixer Link connected, a Trial started, Pro bought. Each install has a random ID. Never sent: recordings, Show names, notes, channel names or file names. Settings › Share crash reports and usage turns it all off and deletes the ID. The App Store version sends nothing.
- Folders in the Shows folder that aren't Shows (no Show.json and no Take folders) are no longer listed as a Show dated Dec 31 of year 0.
- Finding the mixer is quicker: once one of its ports answers, the app stops waiting for the other one.

What to try, the riskiest first (all were checked only by tests and CI builds, never on a device):
1. Open Settings. Is "Share crash reports and usage" there, and on? Open the app, record a short Take and stop it. Then turn the switch off, record another, and turn it back on. Tell the developer the time of each, so the events can be matched up.
2. With Mixer Link on an XR18 or X32, how long does it take from opening the app to seeing the channel names? It should be about 1.5 s quicker than in 1.1.18.
3. Record a Take to Both and stop it as usual. Do the Stems, the Show report and the Drive Copy look the same as in 1.1.18?
4. Open the Show list. Does every Show still appear, with the right date?
