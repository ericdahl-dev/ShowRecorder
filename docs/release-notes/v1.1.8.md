New in this build: the new Free and Pro. Free now records every USB Channel. Pro adds what makes a show easier: channel names and colors from the mixer on your Stems, the second Copy on a Drive, unlimited Markers, auto-gain and clip protection. The 14-day Trial still includes everything, so you'll only see the Free limits once your Trial has ended.

Also in this build:
- On Free, a Drive you plug in shows "Drive · Pro" and the Take is recorded on this iPhone; you're offered Pro once, never during a Take.
- On Free, a Take keeps up to 3 Markers; the 4th is refused with a short note under the Marker button.
- After a Free Take with Mixer Link, you're offered Pro once to name the Stems from the mixer.
- Settings has a Contact support row, with your app and device details filled in for you to check before sending.

What to try, the riskiest first:

Free (once your 14-day Trial has ended):
1. Record a Take. Are all the USB Channels there, named USB 01, USB 02 and so on?
2. Plug in a Drive. Does the chip read "Drive · Pro", and does the Take land on this iPhone only? Is the Pro offer shown just once?
3. Drop 4 Markers in one Take. Is the 4th refused, with the note under the Marker button?

Pro or the Trial:
4. Record a Take with Mixer Link on. Do the Stems have the mixer's channel names, and does the Drive get its Copy?
5. Are auto-gain and clip protection there and working?

Everyone:
6. Settings › Contact support. Does Mail open with your app and device details filled in?
