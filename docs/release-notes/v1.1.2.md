Fixed: on iPhone and iPad, Mixer Link couldn't connect in 1.1.1 ("The mixer connection failed ... error 22 - Invalid argument"), even though Find Mixers found the mixer. Connect works again, so channel names and colors come from the mixer as before.

This build is the one to try on a Behringer X32 or Midas M32. If you have one, please try it and tell us what you see, good or bad.

What to try:

1. Connect the mixer's USB card to your iPhone, iPad or Mac, and join the mixer's network.
2. Open Settings, Mixer Link, and Find Mixers. Tap your mixer. Does it connect without an error, and do the meters show the mixer's channel names and colors?
3. Rename a channel or change its color on the mixer. Does the app follow within a second or two?
4. Record a short Take and check the files: one WAV per USB Channel, named as on the mixer.

On an XR18 or MR18, step 2 is the fix to check: Mixer Link should connect on the first try.
