New since 0.1.21:

- Markers and Channel names open as full sheets on iPhone, like Settings. Nothing opens over the meters any more. Close the sheet to get back to Stop and the flag.
- Tapping a channel name on a meter opens a small box to type the name.
- Behind the scenes: more tests for 44.1 and 96 kHz Takes, and App Store preparation. Nothing else changes for you.

What to try:

1. Open Markers, Channel names and Settings, with and without a Take running: does each one cover the whole screen, never just the meters? Is Done easy to find, and is Stop right there after you close it?
2. Tap a channel name on a meter, type a name and save: does the meter show it?
3. In landscape, open Markers: same full sheet?
