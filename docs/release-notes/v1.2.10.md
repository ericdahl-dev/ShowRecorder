New in this build: on the record screen, every channel number in a row is the same size, so with 32 channels all meters end on one line. Everything in 1.2.9 is here too.

New since 1.2.9:
- With 32 USB Channels, numbers 1-9 used to be bigger than 10-32, so meters 1-9 ended a little higher. Now every number in the row uses one size and all meters line up. With 18 channels the numbers stay full size.

What to try, the riskiest first (checked only by tests and CI builds, never on a device or in the Simulator):
1. Pick "Demo signal (32 channels)" on the iPhone, in portrait. Do all 32 meters end on the same line? Can you read every channel number, including 10-32, with none cut off or shown as "…"? Turn the phone to landscape and check again.
2. With the XR18 (18 channels), are the numbers full size and the record screen the same as in 1.2.8?
3. Record a short Take with the 32-channel Demo signal, make one channel clip and leave one out. Do the marks still show in the narrow meters?
4. Same checks on the iPad and the Mac, if you can.
