New since 0.1.16:

- You can take a channel name off. On the meter, tap the number, empty the box and save. On the Channel names page, empty a row or tap its clear (x) button.
- Behind the scenes: groundwork for Pro. Nothing is limited in this build; every USB Channel is still recorded, with the Show report and Reaper project as before.

What to try:

1. Name channel 1 (tap its number), then open it again, empty the box and save. Does the meter go back to its plain number?
2. Settings > Channel names: name two channels, then clear one by emptying the row and the other with the (x) button. Are both gone, on the page and on the meters?
3. Record a short Take with all 18 channels Armed. Are all 18 Stems there, with the Show report and Reaper project?
