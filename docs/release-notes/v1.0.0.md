The 1.0 candidate: this is the build going to App Review.

New since 0.1.23:

- Pro is on. The first launch offers the 14-day Trial. Settings has a Pro section with the plan, See what Pro adds, Start 14-day Trial, Buy Pro and Restore Purchases. After a Trial ends, Free records 2 USB Channels of your choice. In TestFlight, purchases use the sandbox and cost nothing.
- The paywall and the Trial screen no longer close by themselves when opened from Settings.
- The Trial screen names everything Pro adds.
- Wording across the app matches the rest of ShowRecorder, for example "Stop Take 2?" and "Audio interface".

What to try (in this order; purchases stay with your Apple ID):

1. Delete the app and install this build. Does the 14-day Trial offer appear, with the price and every Pro feature? Tap Not now.
2. Record a Take with your mixer. Are only 2 USB Channels recorded?
3. Settings > Pro > See what Pro adds: does the paywall stay open until you close it?
4. Start the 14-day Trial and confirm the sandbox sheet. Does the plan show Trial, with a days-left chip, and does every channel record?
5. Buy Pro (sandbox). Does the plan show Pro?
6. Delete and reinstall, then Settings > Pro > Restore Purchases. Is it Pro again?
