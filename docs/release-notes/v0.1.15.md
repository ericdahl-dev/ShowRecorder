New since 0.1.14:

- Meters you can tell apart in Sunlight. Yellow is a real yellow, bars have a black edge, green, yellow and red differ in lightness, the target band has visible edges, and the average bar no longer blends into the peak bar. Dark and System look as before, with an edge on light screens.
- Drive trouble is louder. If the Drive stops during a Take you get a red alert with a Check Drive button, and a green notice when it is back ("The Gap will be Repaired after the Take"). Having no Drive from the start stays a quiet warning.
- Settings during a Take. Appearance, the landscape button side and Channel names can now be changed mid-Take. Input, Pre-roll, Drive and Mixer Link stay locked.
- Say why Record is dim. After an idle disarm, a failed input or no microphone access, the screen says so and offers an Arm button. Arming only arms; it never starts a Take.
- End Show is easy to find. Tap the Show line (it has a small arrow now): the sheet is called Show and End Show comes first.
- Names the Mixer sends late. If the Mixer Link comes up during a Take, the files take the real names when the Take ends. A name you typed still wins.
- Delete sheets are clearer. A Copy that does not exist says "Not there", Delete is red, and deleting one Take or one channel file asks a plain question (typing the name is only for a whole Show).
- Show list: a Share button on every row, and the Copy line only appears when something is wrong ("No Drive copy", "Drive not connected now", "Drive copy has Gaps").
- Fixed: a Show with a very long name could fail to start. Names are now cut to a safe length, and the Show line is one line.
- The Take menu on iPad keyboards: Command-R record, Command-period stop (asks first), Shift-Command-M marker.

What to try:

1. Record a Take with the meters in Sunlight (Settings > Recording > Appearance). Can you read green, yellow and red at a glance, and tell the thin average bar from the wide peak bar?
2. With a Drive connected, start a Take and pull the Drive out. You should see a red "The Drive stopped" alert with Check Drive. Plug it back: a green "The Drive is back" notice.
3. In the middle of a Take, open Settings and change Appearance. Input should be locked.
4. Leave the app armed for 30 minutes, or deny the microphone in iOS Settings: Record should say why and offer Arm.
5. Type a very long Show name (more than 100 characters) in the Show sheet and start a Take.
6. Open the Show list: tap the share button on a row.
