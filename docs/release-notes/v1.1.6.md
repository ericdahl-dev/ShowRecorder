New in this build: auto-gain. With Mixer Link connected to an XR18 or MR18 (or an X32 or M32), ShowRecorder can set each channel's preamp for you, bringing it to -18 dBFS while keeping the mix the same. Please try it before anything else.

How it works: tap Gain on the record screen and play into the channels. Auto-gain listens for a few seconds, then moves each preamp in steps. To keep the mix the same, it moves the fader, the pre-fader sends and the gate and compressor thresholds the other way. A gate or compressor that's off, or set where it does nothing, is left alone. Undo puts everything back. It never runs during a Take.

What to try, the riskiest first:

Auto-gain (Mixer Link connected, the mixer Armed over USB):
1. Tap Gain, play steady signal into a channel, and tap Auto-gain on that channel. Does it land near -18 dBFS, and does the main mix stay at the same level?
2. Tap Undo on that channel. Are the preamp, fader and sends back where they were?
3. With several channels playing, tap Auto-gain all channels. Does each one say what it did, or why it was skipped?
4. On a channel with its gate or compressor switched on, run auto-gain. Is the gate or compressor still doing the same job afterward?
5. Start a Take while auto-gain is running. Does it stop cleanly, and is nothing moved during the Take?
