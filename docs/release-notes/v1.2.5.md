New in this build: arming a channel takes the first tap, and a 2-input interface records one stereo file.

New since 1.2.4:
- The "Arm channel N" and "Don't arm channel N" menu, from a long press on a channel's meter, no longer drops taps. The meters used to rebuild that menu many times a second while it was open.

From 1.2.4, still to check:
- Stereo recording (TestFlight builds only). With an interface that has exactly two USB Channels and both armed, each Take is one stereo file, Stereo.wav, instead of two mono files. Arm only one of the two and you get a mono file as before.

What to try, the riskiest first (checked only by tests and CI builds, never on a device or in the Simulator):
1. Long-press a channel's meter and tap "Don't arm channel N", then "Arm channel N". Does each tap work the first time, with signal moving on the meters? Do the meters still move smoothly?
2. Connect a 2-input USB interface, arm both channels and feed each side a different sound. Record a Take of about a minute with a Drive connected, and add a Marker partway through. Optional: pull the Drive in the middle and plug it back in.
3. Stop. On the device and on the Drive, does the Take hold only Stereo.wav? Does it play as one stereo file, left on the left, with the Marker in place?
4. Open the Show's Reaper project. Is there one stereo item? Does the Show report list one channel?
5. Leave one channel out and record again. Do you get one mono file?
