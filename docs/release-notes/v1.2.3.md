New in this build: choose which channels record, and the app asks for an App Store rating after a couple of good Takes.

New since 1.2.2:
- Choose which USB Channels record (Pro or the Trial). Long-press a channel's meter on the record screen and choose "Don't arm channel N". A channel that isn't armed gets no file, and a Template keeps the choice.
- After your second clean Take, the app may ask for a rating when the Show list appears. Apple shows nothing in TestFlight, so you won't see it here.
- Usage reports (when "Share crash reports and usage" is on) now include repeated taps in one spot on the screen, with where they landed and no text or names, so we can find buttons that don't respond.

What to try, the riskiest first (checked only by tests and CI builds, never on a device or in the Simulator):
1. Long-press a channel's meter and choose "Don't arm channel N" for one or two channels. Record a short Take. Is there no file for those channels, and do the other files keep their channel numbers?
2. Arm them again and record another Take. Are all channels back?
3. Save a Template with a channel left out, pick it again later, and get Ready. Is the same channel still left out?
4. Record two short Takes that stop normally, then open the Show list. Nothing should appear in TestFlight, and nothing should get in the way.
