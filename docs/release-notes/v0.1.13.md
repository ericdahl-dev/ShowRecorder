New since 0.1.12:

- Appearance. In Settings > Recording there is a new Appearance setting: System, Dark and Sunlight. Dark is a dark screen for a dark room. Sunlight is a white screen with black text and strong colors, for a bright stage or outdoors. The Marker flag, status chips, banners and meters all change with it.
- Safer clean-up. When you delete only the Device copy of a Show that is also on your Drive, the app now first checks the Drive copy (every Take and channel present, same audio, no Gaps). The Delete button stays off until the check passes.

What to try:

1. In Settings, switch Appearance to Sunlight and look at the record screen mid-Take, ideally outside or near a window. Can you read the meters, chips and the Marker button? Then try Dark in a dim room.
2. Record a short Take with a Drive connected, then on the Show list swipe the Show left, choose Delete..., turn off the Drive copy, tap "Check the Drive copy" and delete the Device copy. Use a throwaway Show.
3. Same, but unplug the Drive first: the check should say the Drive isn't available and nothing should be deleted.
4. Check all 18 meters fit on the iPad and Mac without scrolling.
