New in this build: deleting Shows is easier, and you can clean up several Shows at once. Everything in 1.2.11 and 1.2.12 is here too, including Mixer Link for the Behringer WING (for testers with a WING).

New since 1.2.10:
- Deleting a Show asks once, "Delete for good?". There's no Show name to type any more.
- The Show that's still open can be deleted from its delete sheet: the button reads "End and Delete" and ends the Show first. You no longer have to tap End Show before deleting.
- A Show whose files were removed in the Files app can be deleted, and the sheet's "None, nothing to delete" no longer looks like the reason you can't.
- Clean up: in the Shows list, tap Select, pick several Shows (each shows its size), or tap "Select empty" for Shows with nothing left in them, then Delete. Settings > Storage > Free Up Space… opens the same thing. Deleting removes the Shows from the iPhone and the Drive, for good.
- Swiping left on a Show and tapping Delete… no longer crashes.
- Mixer Link for the Behringer WING, and a Mixer Link Connect that keeps trying until the Mixer answers.

What to try, the riskiest first (all checked only by tests and CI builds, never on a device or in the Simulator):
1. Record a short Take with the iPhone mic, then without tapping End Show, open Shows, swipe left on that Show and tap Delete…. Does the button read "End and Delete"? Does it delete after one "Delete for good?" and leave the record screen ready for a new Show?
2. Shows > Select: pick two Shows, check the sizes add up, and Delete. Try "Select empty" too. The open Show can't be picked.
3. Settings > Storage > Free Up Space…: does it open the Shows list ready to select?
4. Delete one Take of a Show from inside the Show: it still asks once, as before.
5. With an XR18 or X32: record a short Take as usual. Does Mixer Link still find it and name the channels? With a WING, see the 1.2.11 list.
