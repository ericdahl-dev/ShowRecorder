This build changes how a Drive joins a Take that's already recording, and how a Take recovers when the Drive is pulled and plugged back in. Please try those first.

Also in this build:
- A Drive pulled during a Take and plugged back in no longer loses the last two seconds before it was pulled: the Gap starts where the Drive last saved, and Repair fills all of it.
- Plugging a Drive in during a Take is steadier.
- Mixer Link follows the input you Arm and reconnects more reliably.
- After any Take ends, the Pro offer can appear; never after a Soundcheck Take.

What to try, the riskiest first:

The Drive during a Take:
1. Start a Take with the Drive plugged in. About 10 seconds in, pull the Drive. Wait 10 seconds, plug it back in, record a little longer, then stop. After Repair, does the Drive's copy match the iPhone's, with no silent stretch?
2. Start a Take with no Drive, then plug the Drive in partway. After Stop and Repair, is the whole Take on the Drive?
3. Plug the Drive in and press Stop within a second. Does the app stay steady, and is the Take on the iPhone complete?

Mixer Link (XR18 or another linked mixer):
4. Arm with Mixer Link on, then Arm another input (the iPhone's mic) and back. Does Mixer Link follow the input you Armed?
5. Quit and reopen the app. Does Mixer Link reconnect on its own?
6. Lock the iPhone during a Take with Mixer Link on, then unlock it. Is Mixer Link still connected?
