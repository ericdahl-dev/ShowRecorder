New in this build: pressing Record no longer freezes the screen on a slow USB stick, and Repair and Drive Copies are steadier. Free records every USB Channel; Pro adds names and colors from the mixer, the second Copy on a Drive, unlimited Markers, auto-gain and clip protection. The 14-day Trial includes everything.

New since 1.1.14:
- Pressing Record no longer freezes the screen while the app waits on the Drive. The button shows "Starting…" for at most about 1.5 seconds. A Drive that is slower than that starts the Take on this iPhone with the note "The Drive was too slow to start the Take, so this Take started on the Device only." and joins when it is ready.
- A Stop while "Starting…" (from the watch) cancels the start: no Take is left, and the next one is Take 01.
- During a Take, Markers, Gaps and Dropouts are saved in the Take's details without freezing the screen on a slow Drive.
- Names typed during a Take are put on its files after Stop without freezing the screen.
- Drive only: a Take left on this iPhone is no longer re-read on every move, and a move waits until the Drive has finished writing before it moves that Take.
- A Drive Copy whose write failed is finished the next time the Drive is connected, and Repair fills anything Stop could not confirm.
- Deleting one Take's Device copy checks the Drive first, as deleting a whole Show's does.
- X Air: auto-gain and clip protection move every channel that shares a preamp, and the app reads which channels those are ahead of time.

What to try, the riskiest first (the first four change how a Take starts; Drive only and Both need Pro or the Trial):
1. Slow stick, Drive only, every channel (the 18 channel Take that stuttered before): set Settings › Storage › Record to: the Drive, press Record. Does it say "Starting…" for no more than about 1.5 seconds, then record with the screen staying smooth? A slow stick may start on this iPhone with the "too slow" note. Stop it, plug the Drive back in if asked, and wait for the move. Does the Drive end with the whole Take?
2. A fast Drive (an SSD), Drive only: does the Take start with no noticeable wait, with nothing written to this iPhone?
3. Record to Both with the slow stick. Does the Take start within about 1.5 seconds, and does the Drive Copy join and end complete?
4. From the Apple Watch, start a Take and stop it while the phone says "Starting…". Is no Take left, and does the next Take reuse the same number?
5. Type a channel name during a Take, then Stop. Does the screen stay smooth, and is the name on the files?
6. Pull the Drive mid-Take and plug it back in after Stop. Does Repair fill anything missing on the Drive, with no Gaps left?
7. Drive only, with a Take that this iPhone stood in for and the Drive does not hold in full: end the Show, then open the Show's Delete sheet and delete that one Take's Device copy. Is it refused until the Drive holds all of it?

With an XR18 (auto-gain and clip protection are in this beta only):
8. Put several channels on one preamp, then run auto-gain, and record a Take with clip protection on and push them into clipping. Do all of those channels move together, and does the Take start as usual?
