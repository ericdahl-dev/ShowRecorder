New in this build: Take events and caught failures now reach the developer, so a Take that goes wrong can be seen even when the app doesn't crash. Turn it off in Settings › Share crash reports and usage.

New since 1.2.0:
- Usage now includes each Take: when it starts (number of channels, where it records, the mixer family) and when it ends (its length, any Dropouts, and why it ended: stopped, out of space, no Destination left, or the input changed).
- When the app catches a problem instead of crashing, a report goes to the developer's error server: a Drive Copy that stopped writing during a Take, a Stem that Repair couldn't fill, or a Show the iPhone refused to delete. Never Show names or file names.
- Why a Take ended is now reported in the app's own terms (stopped, out of space, no Destination, input changed).

What to try, the riskiest first (all were checked only by tests and CI builds, never on a device):
1. Record a Take to Both and pull the Drive part way through. Does the Take carry on on the iPhone as in 1.2.0, and does the Show report say what happened? Tell the developer the time, so the Drive report can be matched up.
2. Record two short Takes and stop each one. Tell the developer roughly when, so take_started and take_stopped can be checked.
3. With Settings › Share crash reports and usage off, record a Take. Nothing should be sent.
4. Delete a Show you don't need. Does it go away as before?
