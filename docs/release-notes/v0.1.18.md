New since 0.1.17:

- Find mixers. You no longer need the mixer's IP address: Settings > Mixer Link > Find mixers lists the X-Air mixers on your network. Tap one to connect. It takes a few seconds.
- Alerts you can read in full. Tap the yellow or red banner on the record screen to see the whole message. The banner stays the same size, so the meters don't move.
- A shorter message when fewer channels come in than the mixer sends, such as "Only 1 of the XR18's 18 USB Channels is coming in."

What to try:

1. With your phone on the mixer's Wi-Fi, open Settings > Mixer Link and tap Find mixers. Does your mixer show up with its name and address? Tap it: does the Mixer Link come up?
2. The first time, iOS asks about Local Network access. Allow it, then tap Find mixers again if the first search found nothing.
3. Arm the iPhone microphone with the mixer linked, then tap the banner. Can you read the whole message?
