This build changes what happens at Stop: how Repair fills a Copy's Gaps, and how long Stop waits on a Drive that has stopped answering. Please try those first.

Also in this build:
- Repair at Stop keeps every bit of audio a Copy recorded inside its own Gap, instead of copying the other Copy's audio over it.
- Stop waits on all the Copies at once, at most about 5 seconds, so a pulled or stuck Drive no longer freezes the screen at Stop.
- Swipe a yellow warning banner away; it comes back if the problem changes. Red banners stay until the problem is gone.
- In landscape, a banner takes the place of the status chips at the top, so nothing covers the meters, and the top row and Record button always stay on screen.
- When Mixer Link is connected to a different mixer than the one you Armed over USB, the Soundcheck and the status chip say which.
- Mixer Link tries again when the network port is briefly busy, instead of giving up.

What to try, the riskiest first:

Stop and Repair:
1. Start a Take with the Drive plugged in. About 10 seconds in, pull the Drive, wait 10 seconds, plug it back in, record a little longer, then stop. After Repair, is the Drive's copy complete, with no silent stretch?
2. Start a Take with the Drive plugged in, pull it, and press Stop while it's still out. Does Stop finish within a few seconds, with the Take whole on the iPhone?
3. Record a few ordinary Takes with the Drive in throughout. Does each end normally, on both the iPhone and the Drive?

On screen:
4. Let a yellow warning appear (for example, low battery not charging) and swipe it away. Does it stay away until the problem changes?
5. Turn the iPhone to landscape with a warning showing. Is the warning in the top row, with the meters uncovered and Record on screen?

Mixer Link (XR18 or another linked mixer):
6. Quit and reopen the app. Does Mixer Link reconnect on its own?
