New since 0.1.18:

- Templates. Save a venue's setup (channel names, venue, Pre-roll and mixer) and start a Show from it in one tap: tap the Show line, then a Template. If the mixer's address changed, the app finds it by its name. Mark one as the default (★) and it readies the next Show when the app opens. A past Show can be saved as a Template from its Show page.
- Sample rate. Settings > Recording > Sample rate records at the rate your input offers, 96 kHz included. With a mixer, the mixer sets the rate, so only "Interface" is offered.
- Time left on the Device and Drive now follows the input's real sample rate.
- Behind the scenes: the recorder checks its Drive and Device on its own during a Take, and says why a Take ended. Pro is still hidden: nothing is limited in this build.

What to try:

1. Name a few channels, tap the Show line, and choose "Save this setup as a Template…". Start a new Show from it: are the names, Pre-roll and mixer back?
2. Make that Template the default (swipe it, Make default), close the app fully and open it again: are the names already there? Record: is the Show named after the Template?
3. With the XR18 on USB, open Settings > Recording: does Sample rate show only "Interface (48 kHz)"? On an interface that offers more, try 96 kHz and record a short Take.
4. During a Take, pull the Drive out and plug it back in: does the Drive Copy carry on, and is the message when the Take ends clear?
