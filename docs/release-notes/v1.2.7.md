New in this build: Stop waits for a slow Drive to finish instead of cutting it off, a Help row in Settings, and TestFlight features stay on when the network drops.

New since 1.2.6:
- With a slow Drive, Stop keeps waiting as long as the Drive is still writing, and the record screen says "Saving to the Drive… Don't unplug." until every Stem on it is finished. Before, Stop gave up after 5 seconds and the Stems on the Drive could be left unfinished.
- Settings has a Help row that opens the ShowRecorder docs.
- Going offline mid-run no longer hides the beta features (the Demo signal and the other TestFlight-only features) until the next launch.
- The support email's Diagnostics now says which plan you're on: Free, Trial or Pro.
- Crash reports and usage say Free, Trial or Pro too, when "Share crash reports and usage" is on.

What to try, the riskiest first (checked only by tests and CI builds, never on a device or in the Simulator):
1. Use the slow USB stick as the Drive, 18 channels, Pro. Record a minute, then Stop. Does the screen say "Saving to the Drive… Don't unplug." and wait, and does every Stem on the stick open with its full length afterward?
2. Same setup, but pull the stick while it says "Saving to the Drive". Plug it back in. Are the Stems finished and their Gaps Repaired?
3. With the XR18, record a Take that clips one channel, then one with a channel silent. Do clip protection and the no-signal warning still work?
4. On the iPad, hold to stop, with VoiceOver on if you can.
5. Turn on Airplane Mode, then open Settings > Support. Is the Demo signal still offered?
6. Tap Settings > Help. Do the docs open?
7. Turn "Share crash reports and usage" off, record a short Take, and check that nothing new reaches PostHog.
