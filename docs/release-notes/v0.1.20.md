New since 0.1.19:

- On iPhone, Markers, Channel names and Settings open as a panel over the meters (beside them in landscape), so Stop and Marker stay in reach during a Take. Tapping a channel name opens the panel too, not a box over Stop.
- Controls near Record and Stop are easier to hit (44 pt), and the meters keep their height.
- If the app is closed or crashes during a Take, the next launch finishes that Take: every second of audio that reached the phone is kept, a shorter Drive copy is filled in, and the Show report and Reaper project include it.
- A record press that fails (a full phone, a Drive that won't take files) no longer blocks the next one, and a Drive that fails at the press no longer stops the Take: it records to the phone.
- Settings and other sheets change at once when you pick an Appearance, and their warnings are easier to read in Sunlight and Dark.
- No more "Only 1 of the XR18's 18 USB Channels" warning when you record the iPhone's microphone with the mixer linked over Wi-Fi.
- Recording at 96 or 44.1 kHz no longer stops if the USB connection blips.

What to try:

1. Start a Take, then open Markers, Channel names and Settings: can you always reach Stop and Marker? Tap a channel name: does the panel open?
2. Start a Take, swipe the app away (force-quit) after 20 seconds, and open it again. Is the Take there with all its audio (Shows, then the Show)? Does the Show report list it?
3. Settings > Appearance: switch to Dark with Settings open. Does Settings itself change at once?
4. Record with the iPhone's microphone while the XR18 is linked over Wi-Fi: no yellow warning?
5. During a Take, pull the Drive out and plug it back in: does the Drive copy carry on?
