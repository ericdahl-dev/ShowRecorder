This is the build planned for the App Store as ShowRecorder 1.2. It adds a few small changes to 1.2.13.

New since 1.2.13:
- Crash reports and usage are on in every build, the App Store included. To stop them, turn off Settings > Share crash reports and usage. That also deletes the app's random ID. The privacy policy on showrecorder.app says what is sent.
- Shows > Select and Settings > Storage > Free Up Space… are now in every build, not just TestFlight.
- Settings has a "ShowRecorder on Facebook" link next to Help.
- The Demo signal's meters sit in the green target band, and its peaks never go red.

What to try:
1. Record a short Take as usual, with your mixer or the iPhone mic. The files should be on the iPhone, and on the Drive if you use one.
2. Settings > Share crash reports and usage: the switch is there and on by default. Turn it off and on again.
3. Shows > Select: pick a Show and Delete. It still asks once, "Delete for good?".
