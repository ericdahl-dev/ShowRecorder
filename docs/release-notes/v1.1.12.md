New in this build: the new Free and Pro. Free records every USB Channel. Pro adds channel names and colors from the mixer on your Stems, the second Copy on a Drive, unlimited Markers, auto-gain and clip protection. The 14-day Trial includes everything, so you'll see the Free limits only once your Trial has ended.

New since 1.1.11:
- If a Take records only silence on every channel for about 13 seconds, a banner says so and what to check. The Take keeps recording.
- Mixer Link uses one connection to the mixer instead of two, so connecting is steadier.
- Drive file work (a Drive Take, Repair, Marker renames) no longer holds up the rest of the app when a Drive is slow.
- The Contact support email has your version and build in the subject and a Diagnostics block you can delete before sending.

Also in this build:
- When input is interrupted during a Take, the banner says why: the iPad's built-in mic was muted by a cover, the USB input was disconnected, or another app or a call took it.
- After a Dropout, the silence sits exactly where the audio was lost.
- Hold Stop for a second to end a Take on iPhone or iPad; a tap shows "Hold to stop".
- Green banners fade after 10 seconds; tap × or swipe to close one sooner.
- Place a Marker from the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island.
- The Apple Watch reconnects when you raise your wrist and no longer quits on Record, Marker or Stop.
- The Trial chip reads "Trial · 12 days"; tap it for details.
- Pro shows "Launch price" under Buy Pro.
- On Free: a Drive shows "Drive · Pro" and the Take stays on this iPhone; a Take keeps up to 3 Markers; after a Take with Mixer Link you're offered Pro, at most once a Show.

What to try, the riskiest first:
1. Arm the mixer with nothing playing (or its USB sends muted) and start a Take. After about 13 seconds, does the no-signal banner appear? Play something: does it go away at once?
2. With an XR18 on the network, connect Mixer Link. Power-cycle the mixer: does the Link come back by itself? Then use Find Mixers: is the mixer found and does it connect?
3. Record a Take with a Drive plugged in, then switch to Drive only and record another. Rename a Marker on each. Do both finish, with the Copies and the new Marker names right?
4. Settings › Contact support: does the subject show the version and build, and the body a Diagnostics block with your input and Mixer Link?
5. Close the iPad's cover during a Take, then unplug and replug the USB cable. Does each banner say why, with the gap marked as a Dropout?
6. Hold Stop to end a few Takes. Is every Stem saved?
7. Let the watch sleep during a Take, then raise your wrist. Does it show the Take within a second or two, with the iPhone locked too?
8. Lock the iPhone during a Take and place a Marker from the Lock Screen.

Free (after the Trial):
9. Are all the USB Channels recorded, named USB 01, USB 02 and so on?
10. Does a Drive read "Drive · Pro", and does a 4th Marker place nothing?

Pro or the Trial:
11. With Mixer Link on, do the Stems have the mixer's names, and does the Drive get its Copy?
