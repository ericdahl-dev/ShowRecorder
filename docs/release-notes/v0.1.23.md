New since 0.1.22:

- Soundcheck: a 30-second check of your rig. It is offered once on the record screen, then lives in Settings under Run Soundcheck. It ends the open Show, records a short Take in its own "Soundcheck" Show, and checks four things: the Mixer Link, the USB Channels and their signal, the Take on each Destination, and its report and Reaper files. Anything that fails says what to fix.
- The peak bar falls back more slowly, 20 dB a second instead of about 42, so a hit stays readable for about a second. The average bar and the held-peak line are unchanged.

What to try:

1. With the XR18 on USB and the Mixer Link up, run Soundcheck from the banner or from Settings. Do all four steps pass? If one fails, is the fix it suggests clear?
2. After Done, are you back on the record screen with no Show left open?
3. Run Soundcheck with nothing linked, or the USB interface set below 18/18: does it say so plainly?
4. Watch the meters on a live source: does the peak bar feel right now?
