New since 0.1.13:

- Fixed: choosing the Appearance (System, Dark or Sunlight) in Settings did not always register a tap. It is now three plain rows with a checkmark on the one in use.

What to try:

1. Open Settings > Recording and tap Sunlight, then Dark, then System. The screen should change each time and the checkmark should follow your choice.
2. Close and reopen the app: the choice should still be there.
3. Look at the record screen mid-Take in Sunlight: are the meters, chips and the Marker button easy to read?
