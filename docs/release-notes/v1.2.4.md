New in this build: a 2-input interface records one stereo file.

New since 1.2.3:
- Stereo recording (TestFlight builds only). With an interface that has exactly two USB Channels and both armed, each Take is one stereo file, Stereo.wav, instead of two mono files. Arm only one of the two and you get a mono file as before. Interfaces with more channels are unchanged.

What to try, the riskiest first (checked only by tests and CI builds, never on a device or in the Simulator):
1. Connect a 2-input USB interface, arm both channels and feed each side a different sound, so a swap would show. Record a Take of about a minute with a Drive connected, and add a Marker partway through.
2. Optional: pull the Drive in the middle of the Take and plug it back in.
3. Stop. On the device and on the Drive, does the Take hold only Stereo.wav? Does it play as one stereo file, left on the left, with the Marker in place?
4. Open the Show's Reaper project. Is there one stereo item? Does the Show report list one channel?
5. Leave one channel out and record again. Do you get one mono file?
