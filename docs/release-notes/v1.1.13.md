New in this build: choose where a Take records, and steadier recording to a slow Drive. Free records every USB Channel; Pro adds names and colors from the mixer, the second Copy on a Drive, unlimited Markers, auto-gain and clip protection. The 14-day Trial includes everything.

New since 1.1.12:
- Settings › Storage › Record to: This iPhone, the Drive, or Both. It applies at each Record press, and Templates remember it. With This iPhone chosen and Takes waiting to move to a Drive you plug in, the app asks first.
- A slow or old USB stick no longer makes the screen stutter during a Take.
- A Drive Copy the app had to give up on is finished, and its Gaps Repaired, the next time that Drive is connected.
- A Copy that ran out of space says "Drive full" or "Device full" instead of "stopped".
- On Free, the Drive is never used, not even partway through a Take.
- Repair never writes silence over real audio, and a Drive that rejoins a Take resumes exactly where its files end.
- Clip protection never turns a preamp down more than its 12 dB, and auto-gain's undo no longer reverses a clip protection turn-down.

Also in this build:
- A banner when a Take records only silence for about 13 seconds.
- Mixer Link uses one connection to the mixer.
- The interruption banner says why input stopped.
- Hold Stop for a second to end a Take on iPhone or iPad.
- Green banners fade after 10 seconds; tap × or swipe to close one sooner.
- Place a Marker from the Lock Screen or Dynamic Island.
- The Apple Watch reconnects when you raise your wrist.
- Contact support includes your version, build and a Diagnostics block.

What to try, the riskiest first:
1. Plug in a Drive, set Record to Both, and record a Take with every channel. Does the screen stay smooth, and does the Drive Copy end complete, with no Gaps?
2. Record to the Drive, then pull the Drive mid-Take and plug it back in. Does the Take end complete on the Drive?
3. Pull the Drive right after Stop, then plug it back in and open the app. Does the Drive Copy get finished?
4. Set Record to This iPhone with a Drive connected. Does the Take stay on this iPhone, with the Drive rows dimmed in Settings?
5. With clip protection on, drive several channels on one preamp into clipping. Is the preamp turned down no more than 12 dB in all?
6. Fill a Drive almost to the top and record. Does the chip say "Drive full" while the Take carries on?

Free (after the Trial):
7. With a Drive plugged in, is every Take on this iPhone only, with nothing written to the Drive?

Pro or the Trial:
8. With Mixer Link on, do the Stems have the mixer's names, and does the Drive get its Copy?
