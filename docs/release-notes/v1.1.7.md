New in this build: clip protection. With Mixer Link connected, ShowRecorder can turn a clipping channel's preamp down on the mixer during a Take, moving the fader up by the same amount so the mix stays the same. It's off until you switch it on in Settings › Mixer Link. Please try it before anything else.

How it works: during a Take, when a channel's peak hits the top, clip protection lowers that channel's preamp to put the peak near -6 dBFS and raises its fader to match. Each move adds a Marker to the Take. It moves a channel at most 12 dB per Take, waits a few seconds before moving the same channel again, and never acts between Takes.

Also in this build:
- Swipe away the green summary after a Take, as well as yellow warnings.
- Settings shows the version and build number at the bottom.

What to try, the riskiest first:

Clip protection (Mixer Link connected, the mixer Armed over USB):
1. Switch on Settings › Mixer Link › Clip protection. Start a Take and push one channel until it clips. Is its preamp turned down, its fader up by the same amount, and the main mix the same level? Is there a Marker?
2. Keep that channel clipping. Does it stop after 12 dB in that Take?
3. Turn the preamp back up by hand during the Take and clip again. Does it still stop at 12 dB in total?
4. Stop the Take. Does nothing move between Takes?

On screen:
5. After a Take, swipe the green summary away. Does it stay away until the next Take?
6. Scroll to the bottom of Settings. Does it show 1.1.7 and its build number?
