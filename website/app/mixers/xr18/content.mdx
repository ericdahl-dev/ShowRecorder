## Record without a laptop

Recording an XR18 usually means a laptop on the stage box and a DAW running all night. With ShowRecorder the phone or tablet you already carry does the job, and the mixer's own Wi-Fi gives the files their names. When the show ends, one file per channel is ready to [open in Reaper or your DAW](/docs/daw). How it stacks up against a laptop and the other apps is on the [comparison page](/compare), and the steps are in the guide to [recording a gig without a laptop](/guides/record-gig-without-laptop).

## What you get

- **All 18 USB Channels**, recorded in step with each other at the mixer's sample rate.
- **Files named from the mixer.** Over the Mixer Link, ShowRecorder reads each channel's name and color from the XR18 or MR18 and picks up a rename on the mixer within a second or two: on screen straight away, and with Pro in the files from the next recording.
- **A second copy as you record** with Pro, on a USB drive through a hub, so one failure doesn't lose the show.
- **Markers you drop during the set, and the seconds before you press record**, and with Pro a report of the show and a Reaper project.

## What you need

- A Behringer X Air XR18 or Midas MR18. The XR12 and XR16 send no multichannel USB audio, so they can't be multitracked this way.
- A USB-C iPhone (iPhone 15 or later) on iOS 26, or a USB-C iPad on iPadOS 26, and a USB-B to USB-C cable to the mixer. For a long show, a powered USB-C hub between them keeps it charged. A Mac version is in testing (macOS 26).
- For names and colors: your iPhone or iPad on the mixer's Wi-Fi, or on the network the mixer is connected to. Recording itself needs only the USB cable.

## Set it up

1. Connect the mixer's USB port to your iPhone or iPad. Settings shows **18 USB Channels**.
2. Join the mixer's Wi-Fi.
3. Open **Settings**, then **Mixer Link**, and tap **Find Mixers**. Tap your mixer. The meters take the mixer's channel names and colors.
4. Press record.

Step by step, with what to check: [USB cable and setup](/docs/usb-cable-and-setup) and [Mixer Link](/docs/mixer-link).

## Choose what USB records

ShowRecorder records whatever the mixer sends to each USB Channel. Each USB send's source and tap point are set on the mixer, in **Routing** in X AIR Edit ([X AIR User Manual, section 6.13](https://cdn-media.empowertribe.com/ffe0de45e14541f797928d639a8933b2/M_BE_0605-AAA_X-AIR_EN.pdf#page=23)).

For a recording you will remix, set each send's tap to Input. The [XR18 recording guide](/guides/record/xr18#xr18-tap-points) has the steps and what each tap does.

18 channels at 48 kHz take about 9.3 GB per hour, on the iPhone or iPad and again on the drive if you use one. The record screen shows the time left.

## Free, Trial and Pro

ShowRecorder is free to download, and Free records all 18 USB Channels to your iPhone or iPad, with generic names on the files and up to 3 Markers per recording. With the Mixer Link, the meters show the mixer's names as you record. [Pro](/docs/pricing) adds the names and colors from the mixer on the files, a second copy on a USB drive, unlimited Markers, a report, a Reaper project, 96 kHz, Templates and [auto-gain](/docs/auto-gain). Pro is a one-time {PRO_PRICE_TEXT} purchase at its launch price, with no subscription. Start the 14-day Trial in **Settings › Pro** to try the Pro features.
