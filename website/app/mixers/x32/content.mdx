**X32 and M32 support is in the public beta.** The App Store release of ShowRecorder 1.0 is for the Behringer X Air XR18 and MR18 and other class-compliant USB audio interfaces. The X32 and M32 come to the App Store in a later update. Until then, <a href={TESTFLIGHT_URL ?? "/"}>join the public beta</a> to record yours.

## Record without a laptop

Most X32 and M32 shows are recorded to a laptop running a DAW, or to SD cards. ShowRecorder replaces the laptop with the iPhone or iPad you already carry, and gives you named WAV files when the show ends.

## What you get

- **All 32 USB Channels** from the mixer's USB card, recorded in step with each other, at the mixer's sample rate.
- **Files named from the mixer.** Over the Mixer Link, ShowRecorder reads each channel's name and color, and follows renames as they happen, on the meters and in the next Take.
- **Named after what's really patched.** If you re-patch the card's outputs (a bus or another channel on USB 1), each file is named after what is actually routed to that USB Channel, not after its number.
- **A second copy as you record**, on a USB drive through a hub, so one failure doesn't lose the show.
- **Markers and Pre-roll**, and with Pro a Show report and a Reaper project, as on every mixer.

## What you need

- An X32 or M32 (full size, Compact, Producer or Rack) with a USB card: the **X-USB** card on the X32, the **DN32-USB** on the M32. Using an X-LIVE or DN32-LIVE card? Tell us how it goes.
- A USB-C iPhone (iPhone 15 or later) or an iPad. A powered USB-C hub lets it charge through a long show.
- For names and colors: a Wi-Fi router plugged into the mixer's **Ethernet** port (the X32 and M32 have no Wi-Fi of their own), with your iPhone or iPad on its network. Recording itself needs only the USB cable.

## Set it up

1. Connect the mixer's USB card to your iPhone or iPad. ShowRecorder Arms on it, and Settings shows **32 USB Channels**.
2. Join the network of the router on the mixer's Ethernet port.
3. Open **Settings**, then **Mixer Link**, and tap **Find Mixers**. Tap your mixer. The meters take the mixer's channel names and colors.
4. Press record.

32 channels at 48 kHz take about 16.6 GB per hour on each place a Take is written to. The record screen shows the time left.

## Free, Trial and Pro

ShowRecorder is free to download. Free records 2 USB Channels of your choice, with names and colors from the mixer. The [14-day Trial and Pro](/docs/pricing), a one-time purchase, record all 32. No subscription.
