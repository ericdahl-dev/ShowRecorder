The X32 and M32 work in the public beta now. They come to the App Store in an update after 1.0.

> **Tested only against an emulator so far.** ShowRecorder's X32 and M32 support has run against an X32 emulator, not yet against a real desk. Try it first at a rehearsal, or alongside your usual recorder, before you count on it for a show.

## Record without a laptop

Most X32 and M32 shows are recorded to a laptop running a DAW, or to SD cards. ShowRecorder replaces the laptop with the iPhone or iPad you already carry, and gives you named WAV files when the show ends.

## What you get

- **All 32 USB Channels** from the mixer's USB card, recorded in step with each other, at the mixer's sample rate.
- **Files named from the mixer.** Over the Mixer Link, ShowRecorder reads each channel's name and color, and picks up a rename on the mixer right away: on screen at once, and in the files from the next recording.
- **Named after what's really patched.** If you re-patch the card's outputs (a bus or another channel on USB 1), each file is named after what is actually routed to that USB Channel, not after its number.
- **A second copy as you record**, on a USB drive through a hub, so one failure doesn't lose the show.
- **Song markers and the seconds before you press record**, and with Pro a report of the show and a Reaper project, as on every mixer.

## What you need

- An X32 or M32 (full size, Compact, Producer or Rack) with a USB card: the **X-USB** card on the X32, the **DN32-USB** on the M32. The X-LIVE and DN32-LIVE cards haven't been tested; if you have one, tell us how it goes.
- A USB-C iPhone (iPhone 15 or later) or an iPad. A powered USB-C hub lets it charge through a long show.
- For names and colors: a Wi-Fi router plugged into the mixer's **Ethernet** port (the X32 and M32 have no Wi-Fi of their own), with your iPhone or iPad on its network. Recording itself needs only the USB cable.

## Set it up

1. Connect the mixer's USB card to your iPhone or iPad. ShowRecorder picks it up, and Settings should show **32 USB Channels**. If it doesn't, tell us.
2. Join the network of the router on the mixer's Ethernet port.
3. Open **Settings**, then **Mixer Link**, and tap **Find Mixers**. Tap your mixer. The meters take the mixer's channel names and colors.
4. Press record.

32 channels at 48 kHz take about 16.6 GB per hour, on the iPhone or iPad and again on the drive if you use one. The record screen shows the time left.

## Free, Trial and Pro

ShowRecorder is free to download. Free records 2 USB Channels of your choice, with names and colors from the mixer. The [14-day Trial and Pro](/docs/pricing), a one-time purchase, record all 32. No subscription.

## Where to get it

Not from the App Store yet for the X32 or M32. The App Store version records the XR18 and MR18. X32 and M32 recording is in the public beta now and comes to the App Store in a later update.
