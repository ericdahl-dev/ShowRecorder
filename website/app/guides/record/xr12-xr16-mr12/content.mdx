# How to multitrack record a Behringer XR12, XR16 or Midas MR12

<LastChecked path="/guides/record/xr12-xr16-mr12" />

**Short answer.** You can't. Multitrack means every channel on its own track, and these three mixers can't do it. They have no USB audio interface, the part that sends each channel to a computer, iPhone or iPad. What they can do is record a stereo mix (left and right, 2 tracks) to a USB flash drive plugged into the mixer. If you need every channel, you need an XR18 or MR18: see the [XR18 and MR18 guide](/guides/record/xr18).

**Quick start**

1. **Need every channel on its own track?** Use an XR18 or MR18 instead. ([Ways to record](#ways-to-record-an-xr12-xr16-or-mr12))
2. **Plug a USB flash drive** into the mixer's USB port. ([With no computer](#with-no-computer-to-a-usb-flash-drive))
3. **Check the sample rate.** The mixer runs at 48 kHz unless someone changed it. ([Recording settings](#xr12-xr16-or-mr12-recording-settings))
4. **Press record** on the USB recorder in the app.
5. **Record a 30 s test and play it back** before the show.
6. **Wait for the red light** next to the USB port to go out before you pull the drive out.

Every other mixer is in the [mixer recording guides](/guides/record), and the words used here are in its [glossary](/guides/record#glossary). Facts come from the Behringer and Midas manuals and product pages. The [sources](#sources) are at the end.

## Ways to record an XR12, XR16 or MR12

### To a computer over USB

Not possible. These mixers have no USB audio interface, so a computer doesn't see them as a sound card.

- The Behringer manual: the XR18 has "an 18x18 USB audio/MIDI interface", while "the XR12 and XR16 allow 2-track stereo recording".
- The Midas manual says the same of the MR12: it "allows 2-track stereo recording".
- Their only USB port is Type A, the flat kind a flash drive plugs into. It is not for a cable to a computer.

### With no computer: to a USB flash drive

This is the one way to record on these mixers. You get one stereo WAV file of the mix.

1. **Plug a USB flash drive** into the Type A USB port.
2. **Open the USB recorder** in the app (X AIR Edit for the XR12 and XR16, M AIR for the MR12).
3. **Pick what to record and press record.** The manuals don't list the steps. Behringer's XR12 and XR16 product pages link a short video, "X AIR How To: USB Recorder (X AIR EDIT) XR12 / XR16".
4. **Wait for the red light to go out** before you remove the drive. It lights while the mixer is reading or writing the drive.

{/* Screenshot: the USB recorder in X AIR Edit with a flash drive plugged in. <Screenshot src="/images/guides/xr12-usb-recorder.png" alt="Behringer X AIR Edit USB recorder panel with a flash drive connected and the record button" /> */}

What you get:

- **Tracks:** 2, the left and right of a stereo mix.
- **XR12 file:** stereo WAV, 16-bit, at 44.1 or 48 kHz (Behringer).
- **MR12 file:** WAV, 16-bit, at 44.1 or 48 kHz, matching the mixer's sample rate (Midas).
- **XR16 file:** Behringer says only "stereo files". It is most likely the same as the XR12, since they share a manual and an app (our reading).
- **Drive size and format:** neither maker says.

### To an iPhone, iPad or Mac with an app

Not possible for the channels. No audio goes out over USB, so no app can record them. The iPhone, iPad and Android apps only control the mixer, over Wi-Fi. See [Mac, iPad and iPhone](#xr12-xr16-or-mr12-on-mac-ipad-and-iphone) below.

## XR12, XR16 or MR12 recording settings

- **Sample rate:** 48 kHz unless you change it. To use 44.1 kHz, open the **Setup** page in the app, then the **Audio/MIDI** tab.
- **Mute the main LR fader first** when you change the sample rate. The manuals warn the change can make a pop.
- **Bit depth:** 16-bit on the XR12 and MR12. The XR16's isn't stated.
- **What gets recorded:** the recorder takes its stereo signal from "various sources" (Behringer's block diagram). The manuals don't list them or say where the menu is. The video above shows it.

### Only a stereo file on the stick? That is all these mixers record

That is expected. The XR12, XR16 and MR12 record one stereo mix, never separate channels.

- **To mix the show later,** with each instrument on its own track, you need a mixer with a multitrack USB interface, such as the XR18 or MR18 ([XR18 and MR18 guide](/guides/record/xr18)).
- **They look alike.** The XR12, XR16 and MR12 share the look, the app and the family name with the XR18 and MR18. The difference is the USB audio interface, which only the 18-input models have.

## XR12, XR16 or MR12 USB routing

There is nothing to route: with no USB audio interface, there are no USB channels to send. To choose what the flash drive records, see [Recording settings](#xr12-xr16-or-mr12-recording-settings).

## XR12, XR16 or MR12 playback for virtual soundcheck

A [virtual soundcheck](/guides/record#glossary) plays each recorded channel back through its own fader. These mixers can't do that, because they never recorded separate channels.

- **What they can do:** play a stereo file from the USB flash drive. It comes back on its own stereo "USB" input channel (Behringer's block diagram), so you hear the whole mix at once.

## XR12, XR16 or MR12 tap points

A [tap point](/guides/record#glossary) is where in a channel the signal is picked up for recording: before or after the EQ, the fader and so on. The full story is in [Pre or post fader](/guides/record/pre-or-post-fader).

- **On these mixers it doesn't apply:** there are no per-channel recordings to tap.
- **The stereo recorder:** neither maker says where its mix is taken, for example before or after the main fader.

## XR12, XR16 or MR12 on Mac, iPad and iPhone

- **iPhone and iPad:** can't record the channels. The apps control the mixer over Wi-Fi only. Record the stereo mix to a flash drive in the mixer instead.
- **Mac:** the same. The mixer doesn't appear as a sound input, so a Mac can't record it over USB.
- **Want every channel on an iPhone?** The XR18 sends all 18 channels over a USB cable, and ShowRecorder has recorded them on an iPhone. See the [XR18 and MR18 guide](/guides/record/xr18).

ShowRecorder (the app this site makes) records every USB Channel from any class-compliant mixer. The XR12, XR16 and MR12 send no audio over USB, so ShowRecorder can't multitrack them. Mixer Link, which reads channel names and colors, works only on the XR18/MR18 and X32/M32.
