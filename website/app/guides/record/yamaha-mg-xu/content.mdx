# How to record a Yamaha MG XU

<LastChecked path="/guides/record/yamaha-mg-xu" />

**Short answer.** You can't multitrack an MG XU. Multitrack means every channel on its own track, so you can mix the show again later.

The MG10XU, MG12XU, MG16XU and MG20XU send only their stereo mix over USB: 2 channels, left and right. That gives you one finished recording of what the room heard. You can't remix it later: a vocal that was too quiet stays too quiet. To record every channel, you need a mixer that sends each one, such as Yamaha's newer MGX: see the [MGX guide](/guides/record/yamaha-mgx).

**Quick start**

1. **Plug the MG XU's USB port** into a computer, or an iPad with Apple's camera adapter. ([Ways to record](#ways-to-record-an-mg-xu))
2. **Set the sample rate in your recording app** and record 2 channels. ([Recording settings](#mg-xu-recording-settings))
3. **Listening to the computer while you record?** On the MG10XU, set the channel 9/10 switch to **TO MON**. ([Recording settings](#mg-xu-recording-settings))
4. **Set your mix before the show.** You record the mix you hear. ([USB routing](#mg-xu-usb-routing))
5. **Record a 30-second test and play it back** before the show (our advice).

Every other mixer is in the [mixer recording guides](/guides/record). Words you may not know are in the [glossary](/guides/record#glossary). Facts come from Yamaha's MG10XU manual and spec sheets unless a line says otherwise. The [sources](#sources) are at the end.

## Ways to record an MG XU

### To a computer over USB: the stereo mix only

Plug a USB cable from the mixer's **USB 2.0** port into the computer. The port is USB type B, the square-ish kind.

- **Channels:** 2 channels to the computer and 2 back, on all four models.
- **What you get:** the stereo mix, never the separate channels.
- **Driver:** Yamaha says it is a standard USB audio device, so a Mac should need no extra software, called a driver (our reading).
- **Quality:** up to 192 kHz and 24-bit on all four models. It is 16 or 24-bit with no driver on the MG10XU (Yamaha's MG10XU spec).

### With no computer

The MG XU records nothing by itself. Yamaha lists no recorder or card slot.

### To an iPhone, iPad or Mac with an app

- **iPad:** Yamaha names the iPad 2 or later, with Apple's camera adapter. It records the stereo mix only.
- **iPhone:** Yamaha doesn't name any iPhone, and no USB-C iPad: not tested yet.
- **Mac:** works like any computer above.

The details are in [Mac, iPad and iPhone](#mg-xu-on-mac-ipad-and-iphone) below.

## MG XU recording settings

**Set the sample rate in your recording app.** The mixer has no sample rate setting of its own (our reading). The manual describes none.

- **Listening to the computer while you record (MG10XU):** set the channel 9/10 **LINE / USB** switch to USB. Then set its **TO MON / TO STO** switch to **TO MON**.
- **Why TO MON:** Yamaha warns that **TO STO** can make a loop, with feedback (a loud howl). With **TO MON**, you hear the computer only in the headphones and MONITOR OUT, not the main speakers, and it isn't recorded.
- **TO STO** is for playing music from a computer into the mix, for example background music or a stream.
- **Computer too loud in the mixer (MG10XU):** press the **PROGRAM** knob five times. Turn it to set -24 dB to 0 dB, then press it again.
- **MG12XU, MG16XU and MG20XU:** we have only read the MG10XU's manual. Check your model's owner's manual for where the computer's sound comes back into the mixer and how to listen to it.

### Only 2 channels? That is all an MG XU sends

- **The MG XU sends one stereo mix,** never separate channels.
- **To mix the show later,** with each instrument on its own track, you need a mixer that sends each channel, such as the [Yamaha MGX](/guides/record/yamaha-mgx).

## MG XU USB routing

There is nothing to route. USB carries the stereo mix, and the STEREO LEVEL knob doesn't change it.

- **The stereo mix** is the main left and right mix you hear.
- **No channel can go to USB on its own.**

## MG XU playback for virtual soundcheck

A [virtual soundcheck](/guides/record#glossary) plays each recorded channel back through its own fader. The MG XU can't, because it never records separate channels.

- **What it can do:** play stereo from the computer back into the mixer (channels 9/10 on the MG10XU). You hear the whole mix at once.

## MG XU tap points

A tap point is where in the channel the recording is taken: before or after the EQ or the fader. The full story is in [Pre or post fader](/guides/record/pre-or-post-fader).

- **On the MG XU there is nothing to pick:** there are no channel recordings to tap.
- **The stereo mix:** USB carries it, and the STEREO LEVEL knob doesn't change it.

## MG XU on Mac, iPad and iPhone

- **iPad:** Yamaha's brochure names the iPad 2 or later, with Apple's iPad Camera Connection Kit or Lightning to USB Camera Adapter. Stereo only.
- **iPhone, or a USB-C iPad:** Yamaha doesn't name them: not tested yet.
- **Mac:** no driver should be needed (our reading). The manual says you may need Yamaha's driver, without naming a system.

ShowRecorder (our app) records every USB Channel from a mixer that needs no extra driver. On an MG XU, that is the 2-channel stereo mix. We haven't tested it with an MG XU yet. For each channel on its own track, see the [MGX guide](/guides/record/yamaha-mgx).
