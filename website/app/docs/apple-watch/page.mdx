# The Apple Watch remote

Your Apple Watch is a remote for ShowRecorder on your iPhone. From your wrist you can start a Take, add Markers and stop. You see whether the iPhone is Ready or recording, and for how long.

The watch records nothing itself. The audio stays on the iPhone.

The watch remote is part of Free. See [Pricing](/docs/pricing).

## What you need

- **An iPhone** running ShowRecorder. The watch remote works with the iPhone, not the iPad.
- **An Apple Watch** paired with that iPhone, on watchOS 26 or later.
- **ShowRecorder on the watch.** It comes with the iPhone app. If it isn't on your watch, install it from the Watch app on your iPhone.

## What the watch shows

- **Ready**: the iPhone is Ready to record.
- **Not Ready**: the iPhone isn't Ready, so the watch can't start a Take.
- **Recording**: the time since the Take started, the Take's name and how many Markers it has.

If something needs attention on the iPhone, the watch shows it in orange and taps your wrist. For example: **iPhone low on space**, **A Copy stopped** or **Mixer Link lost**.

## Start a Take

When the watch shows **Ready**, tap **Record** once.

It starts the Take the same way the Record button on the iPhone does, with the same Pro and Trial rules.

If the watch shows **Not Ready** and **Open ShowRecorder on your iPhone**, open the app on the iPhone and plug in the mixer. The iPhone can't start recording from the background, so it has to be Ready first.

## Add a Marker

While a Take runs, tap the big yellow **Marker** button.

On a watch with double tap, you can also double tap your index finger and thumb to add a Marker.

Your wrist gets a light tap when the Marker is in. On Free, a Take holds up to 3 Markers. After that, the watch says **3 per Take on Free**. See [Markers and Pre-roll](/docs/markers-and-pre-roll).

## Stop a Take

Press and hold the red stop button for about 1 second, until the ring around it fills. Then the Take stops.

A tap does nothing, on purpose. A stray tap in the middle of a song should never end a Take. If you let go before the ring fills, the Take keeps going.

The iPhone doesn't ask again. The hold is your confirmation.

With VoiceOver, select **Stop** and double-tap to stop the Take.

## When a command doesn't go through

- **Sending…**: the watch is waiting for the iPhone to answer.
- **Not confirmed: check the iPhone**: no answer came back. The command may or may not have worked, so look at the iPhone.
- **Too late: the link dropped**: the command reached the iPhone too late, so the iPhone didn't run it. A tap from before a dropout never lands minutes later.

## No link

When you raise your wrist, the watch shows **Connecting…** for a few seconds while it reaches the iPhone.

**No link to iPhone** means the watch can't reach the iPhone right now. It also says **The recording on the iPhone carries on.** A lost link never stops a Take.

If the watch heard from the iPhone before the link dropped, it shows that last status instead, dimmed. A line such as "No link · as of 8 s ago" says how old it is. The buttons are off until the link is back, so you never act on a status that may have changed.
