# Markers and Pre-roll

## Pre-roll

While ShowRecorder is Ready, it keeps the last few seconds of audio in memory. When you press record, the Take starts with that audio, so a count-in or the first note before your press isn't lost.

To set the length:

1. Tap the gear to open **Settings**.
2. Under **Recording**, choose **Pre-roll**: **Off**, **5 s**, **10 s** or **20 s**. The default is 10 s.

The note under the setting says how much memory the Pre-roll uses while Ready. With **Off**, a Take starts when you press record. Pre-roll can't be changed during a Take.

## Add a Marker

During a Take, tap the flag next to the Record button. A Marker is placed at that moment and the line under the buttons confirms it, for example "Marker 1 placed". The flag is dimmed when no Take is running.

Markers are numbered **Marker 1**, **Marker 2** and so on. Each one is saved straight away, so it survives even if the app is closed mid-Take.

On Free, a Take holds up to 3 Markers. [Pro](/docs/pricing) and the 14-day Trial have no limit.

## Rename a Marker

- **During the Take:** tap the list icon at the top of the record screen to open **Markers**, then tap a Marker and type a name, such as the song title.
- **After the show:** open **Shows**, tap the Show, then tap a Marker under its Take.

The new name is written into the files on the Device, and on the Drive when it's plugged in.

## Dropout Markers

If audio is lost during a Take, for example when a phone call takes the input, ShowRecorder keeps silence of the exact length and adds a Marker named **Dropout** at that point. Every file stays in sync. Dropout Markers can't be renamed.

## Where Markers end up

- In every Stem, as Broadcast WAV cue points.
- In the [Reaper project](/docs/daw), on the timeline with each Take's start.
- In the Show's list of Markers under **Shows**.

## A note on times

The **Markers** list counts from when you pressed record. The Stems, the Show report and the Reaper project count from the start of the Pre-roll, a few seconds earlier. Both point at the same moment in the audio.
