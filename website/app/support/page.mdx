## Getting started

1. Connect your mixer's USB audio, or any USB audio interface, to your iPhone or iPad. You need a USB-C iPhone (iPhone 15 or later) or an iPad; a powered USB-C hub is recommended so it charges while you record.
2. Open ShowRecorder. It arms the audio interface and shows a meter for each USB Channel.
3. Press the red button to record, the flag to add a Marker, and the red button again to stop.
4. To keep a second Copy, choose a Drive folder in Settings (an SSD works best). Without one, Takes go to the Device only.

## Common questions

### Where are my recordings?

Tap Shows on the record screen, or open the Files app: On My iPhone (or iPad), ShowRecorder, Shows. Each Show has a folder for each Take, with one WAV file per channel, a report and a Reaper project.

### The channels are called "USB 01", "USB 02".

Open Settings, then Mixer Link, then Find Mixers to get your channel names and colors from the mixer (Behringer X Air XR18 and MR18). With Pro you can also tap Names to type them.

### The Drive was unplugged during a Take.

Recording carries on to the Device. When the Drive comes back it rejoins, and after the Take the missing stretch is filled in from the Device Copy.

### Will a recording ever stop on its own?

Not because of a Trial, a limit or a license: that is the Show-Safe Promise. It stops only if you stop it, or if every place it is writing to runs out of space.
